# Driving contract career

Open **Pause → Contracts** to see your rank, the number of completions until the next rank and the pay for each route. Parcel deliveries, timed couriers and scenic tours share a ladder. Each completed contract counts once, including repeat runs. A failed, abandoned or interrupted contract gives no pay or career progress.

| Completed contracts | Rank | Pay on a 10,000 CR route |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0 | Trainee driver | 10,000 CR |
| 3 | Local driver | 10,500 CR (+5%) |
| 8 | Trusted driver | 11,000 CR (+10%) |
| 15 | Senior driver | 11,500 CR (+15%) |
| 25 | Master driver | 12,000 CR (+20%) |

The bonus multiplies the route's existing base reward, rounded to the nearest credit. The contract that earns a promotion pays the previous rate. The next contract pays the new rate. Contract earnings track wages separately from wants.

The **Earn a driving contract promotion** want pays 1,500 CR at a promotion, or 1,800 CR with Free spirit. At the top rank it leaves the draw pool. Existing local and account saves derive the same rank from parcel, courier and tour completion counts. Loading an existing career pays no past promotion rewards.
