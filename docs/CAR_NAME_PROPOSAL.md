After approval, change display `mk` and `md` only first. Preserve internal IDs, prices, physics, class, ownership, paint/mod keys, records and ghost references. Update visible descriptions, README and display-text assertions together. Do not globally replace strings such as `mini` or `gtr`: they are also stable identifiers.

Review fictional year labels separately; present specifications as game statistics. Review body silhouettes, grilles, lights, badges, plate text and marketing renders as separate assets. Renaming is not a rights-clearance certificate. Search chosen makes and models in target-market trademark registries before commercial adoption; none was cleared by this naming exercise.
