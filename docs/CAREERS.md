# Careers

The Market Kitchen in Old Quay is a career with five ranks. Your rank follows the shifts you have finished and your Cooking level, so it needs nothing saved of its own and nothing new for the account to compare.

| Rank | Shifts finished | Cooking level | Pay per shift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dishwasher | 0 | 0 | 300 CR |
| Prep cook | 3 | 0 | 360 CR |
| Line cook | 8 | 2 | 440 CR |
| Sous chef | 15 | 4 | 540 CR |
| Head chef | 25 | 6 | 660 CR |

Pay is the rank's rate times Cooking's bonus (+3% a level), rounded once. A shift is paid at the rank it was worked at. If finishing it, or the Cooking XP it teaches, reaches the next rank, a PROMOTED notice names the new rank and its pay, and a "Get a promotion" want is completed. The kitchen panel shows your rank and what the next one needs.
