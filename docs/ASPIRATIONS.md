# Aspirations

Six long goals of three steps each, shown in Pause → SKILLS under the skills. A step is worked out from what the save already holds, so nothing new is tracked; the save keeps only how many steps of each have been paid (version 1, 0 to 3 each). Steps are paid in order, once each, whenever the game saves (persist asks after almost every change of progress): 1,500 CR for the first, 4,000 for the second and 10,000 for the third.

| Aspiration | Step 1 | Step 2 | Step 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master chef | Prep cook at the kitchen | Sous chef | Head chef |
| Home maker | 5 pieces in your home | 15 pieces | Home comfort 100 |
| Social butterfly | Someone Friendly (15) | Three Friends (40) | A Best friend (90) |
| Speed demon | Win a cup | Win three cups | Win every cup |
| Renaissance | A skill at level 3 | Three skills at level 5 | A skill at level 10 |
| Go-getter | 10 wants done | 30 wants done | 100 wants done |

Someone you have never talked to doesn't count as a friend, whatever their score. Progress made before aspirations existed is paid at the first save after the update. Cloud progress compares the steps paid; none paid matches a save from before aspirations.
