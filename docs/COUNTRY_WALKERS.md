# Country road walkers

Four anonymous walkers on touch devices, eight on desktop. Each borrows a town figure's geometry and walks a short stretch of country verge, 1.5 m beyond the carriageway. They never enter the friendship roster. Keep clear of junctions, the highway and Old Quay.

Allocate once. Look for one free slot at intervals, off camera and 120 to 300 m from the current driver, walker or sailor. At 300 m return the figure to the pool. Approach in a car and they move further onto the verge. A player impact uses the town's witnessed-crime rule. The existing pistol hit rules and violence setting apply.

Phone check: in AUTO on a physical phone, drive the same country road twice with the performance panel open. Compare frame time with the main build, at the same sharpness and camera angle. In the drawn touch test, time drawing the same view with and without four figures, alternating the order and waiting for the GPU after each draw.
