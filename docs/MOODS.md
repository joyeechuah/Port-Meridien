# Mood and skill XP

The Needs panel shows the current mood and its effect. The game checks mood when a skill earns XP. For example, driving 250 clean metres earns one base XP. Twenty such awards give 23 XP while Content or 17 XP while Tense.

| Mood | Skill XP per 100 base XP |
| --- | --- |
| Happy (a feeling) | 125 |
| Confident, Inspired or Energised (feelings) | 150 in Driving, Photography or Fitness, 100 in each other skill |
| Content | 115 |
| Okay | 100 |
| Tired, Hungry, Bored or Tense | 85 |
| Lonely | 150 Charisma, 85 in each other skill |

Each skill keeps its own fractional XP until it reaches a whole point. The fraction survives mood changes and clears at the level cap or when the page reloads. Only whole points enter the save and cloud progress. Credit rewards and existing level perks keep using the earned skill level.

## Feelings

Something that has just happened lifts you for a few minutes of play. Only the latest feeling counts; the same one again renews its time without a second message. The police (Tense) and a low need come first. With neither, the feeling is your mood. The time counts on the needs' clock, so menus, the garage and Photo Mode don't use it up. Feelings last this session only.

| Feeling | Comes from | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Happy | A friend over, or a gift someone loves | 4 min |
| Confident | Winning a race, a gold time or a cup | 4 min |
| Inspired | A new landmark or a postcard | 3 min |
| Energised | A rest at home with a bed | 4 min |

A message names the feeling, what caused it and its effect. The pause menu's NEEDS button shows the mood. Needs shows the cause and the minutes left; a feeling held back by a low need says it comes once that need is seen to. Pause tips suggest what to make of it.
