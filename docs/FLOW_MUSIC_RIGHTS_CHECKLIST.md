# Google Flow Music — rights and evidence checklist for Port Meridien
Research date: 15 November 2026. This refers specifically to **Google Flow Music**, not older MusicFX or Google Flow video alone. No project music ownership or generation account was inspected here.

## What the official documentation supports
- Google’s **Google AI Pro benefits** help page explicitly says the Flow Music **Plus** plan benefit includes **“Commercial use rights.”** This is positive wording relevant to paid-plan generations, but it does **not** by itself prove that each of Port Meridien's three existing `audio/*.mp3` files was made on that eligible tier, or that the right survives all subsequent use/distribution contexts.
- Google's Flow Music onboarding pages discuss Free, Starter, Plus and Member access. I could not establish an equally explicit, universal commercial licence for **Free** generations from the reviewed Google sources. Do not assume every tier has the same rights.
- The broader Google Flow help says Google does not claim ownership of original output. **No-ownership-claim is not equivalent to an exclusive copyright assignment or a guarantee against similarities to protected recordings.** General terms, account plan terms, third-party rights and applicable law still matter.

## Evidence packet for **each track**
| Field | Record required |
|---|---|
| Asset identity | Exact shipped filename, SHA-256 hash, audio duration, release/version and any later edits |
| Source | Google Flow Music project/song URL or stable identifier; screenshot/export of song detail and generation date/time |
| Input provenance | Original prompt, lyrics, stems, audio reference/upload and any generated revisions; permission/license for each uploaded source |
| Model and plan | Model/version (if displayed), account subscription plan at generation and download, invoices/receipts showing eligible plan, date/time of eligibility |
| Licence | Saved PDF/screenshot of Flow Music's applicable commercial-use wording and linked Google TOS on generation date and intended release date; URLs and version/effective dates |
| Edits and contribution | DAW session or edit log, custom lyrics/melody/instrumentation, human arrangement, collaborator permissions and rights |
| Distribution | Intended use: in-game background audio, storefront trailer, paid download, streaming/upload, redistribution of isolated music/stems; check if terms distinguish these |
| Conflict checks | Similarity check against identifiable songs/melodies and third-party copyrighted input; any takedown/claim history |
| Sign-off | Reviewer, review date, evidence folder path and **approved/not approved/needs clarification** status |

## Commercial shipping gate
1. Identify who generated each track, using which plan and what date. If unknown, **do not claim a licence from current plan benefits for that historical file**.
2. Save the applicable terms and subscription entitlement evidence. Ask Google product support to clarify any ambiguous scope, especially whether embedding in a downloadable paid browser/desktop game is covered by the claimed commercial rights.
3. Confirm uploaded references and lyrics are original or licensed. Replace any track with unclear ownership/source rather than relying on a generic AI-output claim.
4. Keep a soundtrack manifest separate from the code/asset inventory; record hashes of the actual shipped MP3 files.
5. Keep the required AI disclosure evidence for Steam's content survey (if using Steam), and avoid claiming automatic copyright exclusivity over purely generated music.

**Outcome:** Google AI Pro help specifically documents Flow Music Plus **commercial use rights**, but Port Meridien's **actual songs remain unverified**. This document does **not** clear them for sale.
