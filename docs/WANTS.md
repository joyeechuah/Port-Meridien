# Wants

Pause → Needs shows three wants under Mood. New players start with meeting someone, walking 500 m and reaching Friendly. Finishing an active want pays credits directly and replaces just that slot. Replacements are random and exclude all three current wants. One reroll replaces the entire list with three different wants, once per UTC calendar day. Leaving the panel or reloading preserves the list.

| Activity | Qualifying action | CR |
|---|---|---:|
| Meet someone | Speak a first line to an Old Quay resident with zero recorded conversations | 600 |
| Reach Friendly | A conversation takes friendship from below 15 to at least 15 | 1,000 |
| Race | Cross the finish in a race, in any position | 1,200 |
| Kitchen | Serve the final correct order of a shift | 1,000 |
| Landmark | Register a first discovery | 900 |
| Photo | Receive an image from the current Photo Mode capture, including after the daily skill XP allowance | 400 |
| Furniture | Buy and place a piece successfully | 650 |
| Home activity | Successfully use SNACK or PLAY | 500 |
| Walk | Travel 500 actual metres on foot while the want is active. Running counts metre for metre | 750 |
| Skill | Increase any skill's level | 1,500 |
| Repair | Complete a roadside repair or return home with actual damage | 800 |

The version 1 wants record stores three activity IDs, per-activity completion counts, partial walking distance and the last reroll UTC day. Loading repairs invalid slots and discards unrecognised counts. Cloud progress compares completion counts. Empty counts match older saves. A newer reroll from an otherwise equal account save carries its list and partial walking distance. Two disconnected devices can each use a reroll before syncing.
