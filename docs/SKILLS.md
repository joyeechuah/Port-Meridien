# Skills
Open SKILLS from Pause. Each bar shows progress to the next level. Level L needs 25 × L × (L + 1) cumulative XP: 50 for level 1, 2750 for level 10. Each payment uses the level before the activity's award.

| Skill | XP | Per level | At level 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Driving | 30 per completed race and 1 per 250 clean road metres | +2% to the race prize | +20% |
| Cooking | 25 per completed kitchen shift and 5 per successful home snack | +3% shift pay | +30% |
| Charisma | First use of each line per conversation: hello or tip 2, chat or compliment 4, joke 5, rude 1 | +4% to positive conversation friendship gains | +40% |
| Fitness | 1 per 50 m walked; accepted forward running distance counts twice | 3% slower energy loss on foot | 30% slower |
| Photography | 10 per completed capture, first five per UTC day | +2% first-discovery landmark credits | +20% |
| Handiness | 15 per confirmed roadside repair or return-home repair of actual damage | 3% off a roadside repair | 30% off |

Time trials, cup completion bonuses and the passport completion bonus retain their existing payouts. Sub-threshold distance is session-local. A collision or leaving the road clears the unfinished clean-driving interval. Credits are rounded once at payment. Garage refunds and car sales award no skill XP. Whole-save cloud selection includes skill XP. The daily photo allowance is saved separately and reconciled on equal progression; an offline device cannot enforce a shared real-time limit across devices.

## Traits
Under SKILLS, choose up to two of the eight traits and press APPLY TRAITS. You can change them once per UTC day. A skill's trait adds 25% to the XP that skill earns.

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Foodie | Cooking XP +25%. Hunger falls 15% faster. |
| Gearhead | Driving XP +25%. Roadside repairs cost 10% less, after Handiness's discount. |
| Athletic | Fitness XP +25%. Energy falls 10% slower on foot. |
| Outgoing | Charisma XP +25%. Social falls 15% faster. |
| Homebody | A snack, play, rest and a friend over at home restore 20% more. Fun falls 10% faster away from home. |
| Shutterbug | Photography XP +25%. Six rewarded photos per UTC day instead of five. |
| Handy | Handiness XP +25%. A home snack gives 10 Cooking XP instead of 5. |
| Free spirit | Wants pay 20% more. A second reroll per UTC day. |
