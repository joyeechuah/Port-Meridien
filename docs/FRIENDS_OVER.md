# A friend over

Anyone you are Friends with (friendship 40 or more) can be asked over while you talk: **Come over to my place** (key 8), beside **Come for a ride**. Asking someone else instead replaces the first invitation. The invitation lasts for this session.

At home, your guest stands on the mezzanine in front of or beside one of your seats, or by the railing if you have none, on a free half-metre cell. They can be seen from the garage and from DESIGN HOME. **HANG OUT · NAME** in the garage then gives:

- social +30 and fun +10
- 4 Charisma XP (with your mood's effect)
- friendship +4, plus 1 for every 25 points of home comfort (up to +8 in a home of comfort 100)
- a conversation counted with them, and a line in their own manner

Then they go home. Hanging out once is all an invitation gives.
